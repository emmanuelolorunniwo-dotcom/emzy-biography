Who Is EMZY?

Olorunniwo Emmanuel Feranmi, popularly known as EMZY BIG STEEZ, is a young Nigerian with big dreams, a creative mind and a passion for building a meaningful future.

I was born in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria, and I grew up in Rivers State. My state of origin is Osun State, specifically Ilesa West Local Government Area.

I believe in learning, growing, discovering my talents and working towards the future I want. My story is still being written, and I believe the best chapters are yet to come.

The Story Behind My Name

The name EMZY came from my real name, Emmanuel. During my secondary school days, my classmates started calling me Emzy, and the name became part of my identity.

Then came BIG STEEZ! It represents confidence, personal style, boldness and that special big-boy energy. 😹

Together, EMZY BIG STEEZ became the personal brand I proudly identify with.
